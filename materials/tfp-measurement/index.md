# TFP Measurement

## Purpose
This section collects frameworks and practical recipes for measuring productivity (TFP) in firm- and plant-level data, with an emphasis on identification, measurement error, and implementation choices.

## Core topics
- **Production function estimation:** OLS pitfalls, simultaneity, selection
- **Classic approaches:** Olley–Pakes (OP), Levinsohn–Petrin (LP), Ackerberg–Caves–Frazer (ACF)
- **Control-function logic:** proxy variables (investment/materials), timing assumptions
- **Data construction:** deflators, value-added vs gross output, capital measurement, labor quality
- **Diagnostics:** monotonicity, weak identification, sensitivity to proxies and deflators
- **Outputs:** firm/plant TFP, industry aggregates, dispersion, reallocation decompositions
